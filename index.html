What to check before ordering

  1. Match the exact listing. Use the item ID to distinguish similarly named products. Check the selected color, size and variant price on the source page.
  2. Compare measurements. Check a shoe listing for foot length in centimeters. For clothing, compare the chest, sleeve and body measurements with a garment that fits you. Review listing photos and any available quality-check photos for the same variant.
  3. Confirm the total. Check whether your buying agent accepts the source listing. Compare the item price, shipping quotes, service fees and any applicable taxes before paying. Check whether a bulky item ships by actual or volumetric weight.

Frequently asked questions

What is this JoyaGoo spreadsheet?

It is an independent product discovery guide with a searchable selection from HipobuyFind. The product links and both shopping buttons lead to HipobuyFind.

Can I use a listing with JoyaGoo?

Open the HipobuyFind product page to look for the original seller URL. If one is available, check it in JoyaGoo and confirm support for that listing and variant before ordering.

Why might the USD checkout price differ?

The catalog shows approximate USD equivalents for the listed item prices. The checkout total can change with the selected variant, the exchange rate, shipping and any applicable fees or taxes.
